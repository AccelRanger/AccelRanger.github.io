Who Am I

The Engineer
Behind the Stack

I'm AccelRanger — a multidisciplinary engineer and founder based in Slovakia, operating at the intersection of hardware and software. My work spans the full technological spectrum: from designing PCB boards and programming PLCs to architecting high-performance server infrastructure and writing efficient algorithms.

In the field of Industrial Automation & Robotics, I design and deploy systems that streamline production lines, integrate sensor networks, and build HMI visualization dashboards that give operators real-time control and clarity.

On the software side, I craft backend systems, automation scripts, and embedded firmware — always with a focus on reliability, performance, and precision. Whether it's a custom CPU instruction set, a server deployment pipeline, or a Lua game server, I approach every challenge with the same engineering discipline.

I founded AccelSystems to bring enterprise-grade technical solutions to clients who demand more — combining deep hardware expertise with modern software architecture to deliver systems that simply work.
